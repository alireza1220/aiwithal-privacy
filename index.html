AI with AL – Instagram comment replies: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This app is used only by the Instagram account @aiwithal_persian (AI with AL / DrMounesi). It sends a free guide to people who ask for it under a post or in a direct message.

What we read

Comments and replies on @aiwithal_persian's own posts, and direct messages sent to @aiwithal_persian: the Instagram username, the text and the time.

Why

To send each person who asked for the guide one direct message with the link, and to make sure nobody gets the same message twice.

What we keep

A short list: the username, the comment or message text, the time, and whether the message was sent. It is stored privately on the account owner's computer. It is never sold, shared or used for advertising.

How long

Only as long as each campaign needs it, and never longer than 90 days.

Deleting your data

To have your data deleted, send a direct message to @aiwithal_persian on Instagram with the word "delete" (or «حذف»). We delete it within 30 days and confirm by message.

Contact

Instagram: @aiwithal_persian
